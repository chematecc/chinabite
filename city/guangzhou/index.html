Guangzhou 广州

Morning dim sum in Guangzhou — order a basket of har gow first. Tea is charged separately.

3 must-eat dishes

Roast Goose (烧鹅) — ¥52 · ≈$7.32 · Spice 0/3
Sold by the whole or half goose. Ask the price first. Crispy skin, eat while hot.

Shrimp Dumplings (Har Gow) (虾饺) — ¥28 · ≈$3.94 · Spice 0/3
Contains shrimp. Shrimp allergy — skip it. Eat while hot.

Rice Noodle Roll (Cheung Fun) (肠粉) — ¥20 · ≈$2.82 · Spice 0/3
Ask about the filling first. Add soy sauce to your taste.
