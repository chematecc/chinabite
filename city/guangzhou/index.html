Guangzhou

Morning dim sum in Guangzhou — order a basket of har gow first. Tea is charged separately.

3 must-eat dishes

Roast Goose (烧鹅) · Spice 0/3
Sold by the whole or half goose. Ask the price first. Crispy skin, eat while hot.

Shrimp Dumplings (Har Gow) (虾饺) · Spice 0/3
Contains shrimp. Shrimp allergy — skip it. Eat while hot.

Rice Noodle Roll (Cheung Fun) (肠粉) · Spice 0/3
Ask about the filling first. Add soy sauce to your taste.

Getting around

Metro to Beijing Road or Shangxiajiu, then walk — food is everywhere.

Tip: Dim sum is charged per seat, tea is extra. Order one basket at a time and try a few.
