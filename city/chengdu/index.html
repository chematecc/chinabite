Chengdu 成都

Wandering Kuanzhai Alley and hungry — sit down, order three dishes, and say 'mild spice' first.

3 must-eat dishes

Mapo Tofu (No Meat) (麻婆豆腐（无肉版）) — ¥26 · ≈$3.66 · Spice 2/3
The no-meat version is still spice 2/3. Say 'mild' if you can't take heat. Ask about the oil and broth.

Twice-Cooked Pork (回锅肉) — ¥38 · ≈$5.35 · Spice 2/3
Pork belly, oily and spicy. Best with plain rice.

Dan Dan Noodles (担担面) — ¥20 · ≈$2.82 · Spice 2/3
Contains peanut sauce. Peanut allergy — skip it.

Getting around

Metro to Chunxi Road or Kuanzhai Alley, then walk — food is everywhere.

Tip: Most Chengdu food is spicy. Say 'mild' or 'no chili' before ordering. For hot pot, start with clear broth.
