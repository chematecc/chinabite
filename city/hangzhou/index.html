Hangzhou 杭州

Tired from walking around West Lake, sit down and order lion's head meatballs with plain rice.

3 must-eat dishes

Lion's Head Meatballs (狮子头) — ¥38 · ≈$5.35 · Spice 0/3
Big pork meatballs, a bit oily. Best with plain rice.

Fox Nut (Gorgon Fruit) (鸡头米) — ¥30 · ≈$4.23 · Spice 0/3
Fox nut is a seed, not chicken. Seasonal — ask if they have it.

Douhua (Tofu Pudding) (豆花) — ¥10 · ≈$1.41 · Spice 0/3
Douhua comes sweet or savoury — ask before ordering.
