Hangzhou

Tired from walking around West Lake, sit down and order lion's head meatballs with plain rice.

3 must-eat dishes

Lion's Head Meatballs (狮子头) · Spice 0/3
Big pork meatballs, a bit oily. Best with plain rice.

Sweet Osmanthus Lotus Root (桂花糖藕) · Spice 0/3
Sweet lotus root stuffed with sticky rice — a sweet vegetarian dish.

Douhua (Tofu Pudding) (豆花) · Spice 0/3
Douhua comes sweet or savoury — ask before ordering.

Getting around

Metro to Longxiangqiao station, walk to West Lake.

Tip: Hangzhou food is sweet and light. Restaurants by West Lake are pricey — ask the price first.
