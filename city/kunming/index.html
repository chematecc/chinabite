Kunming

In the morning in Kunming, start with a bowl of guoqiao rice noodles — the hot broth cooks the meat slices.

3 must-eat dishes

Guoqiao Rice Noodles (Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles) (过桥米线) · Spice 0/3
The hot broth cooks the meat slices. Eat while hot. Meat-stock broth.

Liangfen (Starch Jelly) (凉粉) · Spice 1/3
Slightly spicy, vegetarian. Order it first at night markets.

Douhua (Tofu Pudding) (豆花) · Spice 0/3
Douhua comes sweet or savoury — ask before ordering.

Getting around

Metro to Nanping Street station, walk around Nanping Street.

Tip: Kunming food is sour and spicy. Night-market menus are mostly Chinese — keep your diet card ready. Ask if mushrooms are farmed.
