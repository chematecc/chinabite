Beijing

In the morning in Beijing, grab a jianbing from a street stall and eat as you walk.

3 must-eat dishes

Peking Duck (北京烤鸭) · Spice 0/3
Duck served with pancakes. Sold by the whole duck — ask the price first.

Zhajiang Noodles (炸酱面) · Spice 0/3
Salty soybean sauce. Mix well before eating. Pork sauce.

Jianbing (Chinese Crepe) (煎饼果子) · Spice 1/3
Ask if it has meat or chili. Made fresh at street stalls.

Getting around

Metro to Wangfujing or Qianmen, then walk — food is everywhere.

Tip: Portions are big — two dishes is enough for two people. Duck is sold by the whole bird; ask the price first.
