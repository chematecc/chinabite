Beijing 北京

In the morning in Beijing, grab a jianbing from a street stall and eat as you walk.

3 must-eat dishes

Peking Duck (北京烤鸭) — ¥98 · ≈$13.80 · Spice 0/3
Duck served with pancakes. Sold by the whole duck — ask the price first.

Zhajiang Noodles (炸酱面) — ¥22 · ≈$3.10 · Spice 0/3
Salty soybean sauce. Mix well before eating. Pork sauce.

Jianbing (Chinese Crepe) (煎饼果子) — ¥12 · ≈$1.69 · Spice 1/3
Ask if it has meat or chili. Made fresh at street stalls.
