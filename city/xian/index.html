Xi'an 西安

In the morning on Huimin Street in Xi'an, look for the green halal sign first, then order a bowl of yangrou paomo.

3 must-eat dishes

Lamb Soup with Crumbled Bread (羊肉泡馍) — ¥35 · ≈$4.93 · Spice 0/3
Tear the bread into small pieces yourself — small pieces soak up the flavour.

Roujiamo (Pork Bun) (肉夹馍) — ¥12 · ≈$1.69 · Spice 0/3
Roujiamo is pork. Halal shops use beef — ask before ordering.

Oil-Splashed Noodles (油泼面) — ¥20 · ≈$2.82 · Spice 2/3
Spice 2/3. Say 'less chili' if you can't take heat.

Getting around

Metro to Bell Tower station, walk to Huimin Street.

Tip: On Huimin Street, look for the green halal sign before going in. Lots of lamb and beef — ask about pork dishes first.
