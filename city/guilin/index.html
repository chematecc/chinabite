Guilin 桂林

Tired from walking West Street in Yangshuo at night, sit down for a bowl of Guilin rice noodles.

3 must-eat dishes

Guilin Rice Noodles (桂林米粉) — ¥18 · ≈$2.54 · Spice 1/3
The braised sauce has pork, served with peanuts. Ask first if allergic.

Cold Wood Ear Salad (凉拌木耳) — ¥18 · ≈$2.54 · Spice 1/3
Cold wood ear, slightly spicy, vegetarian. Good with rice.

Sour Plum Drink (酸梅汤) — ¥10 · ≈$1.41 · Spice 0/3
A bit sweet. Good with spicy food.

Getting around

High-speed train to Guilin station, then taxi. Yangshuo has no metro — take a bus or taxi to West Street.

Tip: On Yangshuo West Street, ask the price first. Beer fish isn't on this list — ask how it's cooked and what it costs.
