Lo Mein 捞面

Lāo miàn · Not spicy (0/3)

What's in it

Soft egg noodles, chicken/pork/shrimp, vegetables, soy-sesame sauce

Heads up: 捞面，面是小麦鸡蛋面。配料做法不一：鸡肉/猪肉/虾/素，先问。酱含大豆、芝麻油，有的配鸡汤。

配料先问再点；面软，区别于炒面

Overseas version — preparation may differ. Ask to confirm.
