Questions?

Short answers, no fluff — ask Bun and he'll answer these too▾
How do I order with a QR code?

Scan the code on the table, pick your dishes, pay first — then they cook. Most restaurants work this way, 80%+ of them.

What if the QR code won't open?

Don't wait. Show the waiter the order slip — they'll order on their phone and you pay at the counter.

Dianping or Meituan — what's the difference?

Same company, two jobs. Dianping = choose — scores, photos, price per person, the Must-Eat List. Meituan = get — delivery to your hotel, groceries too. The QR code at your table opens the restaurant's own mini-program, not Dianping.

How do I get food delivered to my hotel?

Best route: the Alipay mini-program → 淘宝闪购 (was Ele.me). Meituan second. The address has to be in Chinese — copy it from your booking, hotel name + room number. Riders stop at the lobby, so ask them to leave it at the front desk. Your hotel card is in the app — fill it once, copy it whenever you order.

Do I need a Chinese phone number?

Not to look around. Without a +86 number you can still browse lists, see photos, check prices and save places offline. You need a login for delivery, queue numbers and group vouchers. Buttons can switch to English — names, menus and reviews stay Chinese.

How do I pay?

WeChat Pay or Alipay first. Cash at the counter works too. Fast food: pay first. Sit-down: pay after. Tableware is often ¥1–5 per person, extra.

How many free checks do I get?

3 free checks a day. A friend's link gets you 6. Watch a 5-second ad for +3 more, up to 5 ads a day. The Kit is $6.99 for 90 days, unlimited, no ads.

What's in the Kit, and how much is it?

$6.99, one time, 90 days. 110 dishes with photos and prices, 12 diet cards, 10 cities, QR ordering help. No ads, no subscription.

How does inviting friends work?

Share your link — friends get 6 free checks, and you get +3 for each friend who finishes their first check. Honest note: there's no server checking this yet, so sharing isn't the same as a verified invite.

Are the restaurant addresses verified?

No. The city lists show the type of place and the area — not a verified street address. Dianping has the real one.

Where can I find you?

We're #ChinaBite on Instagram, TikTok and X — search any dish name there. Every dish has its own page too: chinabite.chinavisacheck.com/dish/mapo-tofu/

Are the prices exact?

No — rough guides from 2026-11-26, about ¥7.1 to the dollar. The basis is a mid-range local restaurant in a Tier-1 city (Shanghai / Beijing). Street stalls are cheaper, hotels and tourist spots cost more. Menus change, so treat them as a ballpark.
