Drinks & Beer

Beer, Coke, tea — and how to ask for it iced.›

Room temp is the default. In China, drinks arrive at room temperature unless you ask. That's normal — not a mistake, and not a warm beer the waiter forgot to chill.

Beer: most restaurants keep beer at room temp. Want it cold? Ask for it ice-cold — the exact words are in the phrase list below.

Coke, Sprite, water: same story — room temp by default. Ask for it iced, or with ice — the words are below.

Tea: hot by default, often by the pot, sometimes free. Iced tea is the exception — the words are below.

Spirits: baijiu is never iced. Red wine comes at room temp. Yellow wine is often served warm.

Say it — the words to use
冰镇啤酒ice-cold beer
冰的iced / cold
加冰with ice
常温room temperature
不要冰no ice
热的hot
一瓶 / 一壶 / 一杯a bottle / a pot / a glass

How do I order with a QR code?

Most restaurants work this way — 80%+ of them. The QR Order card in Orders walks you through it, step by step.

What if the QR code won't open?

Don't wait — the QR Order card in Orders has the backup plan: the slip does the ordering for you.

Dianping or Meituan — what's the difference?

The Delivery tab in Orders explains it where you use it.

How do I get food delivered to my hotel?

The Delivery tab in Orders has the whole flow — the steps, the Meituan / Dianping buttons, and your hotel card.

Do I need a Chinese phone number?

Not to look around. Without a +86 number you can still browse lists, see photos, check prices and save places offline. You need a login for delivery, queue numbers and group vouchers. Buttons can switch to English — names, menus and reviews stay Chinese.

How do I pay?

WeChat Pay or Alipay first. Cash at the counter works too. Fast food: pay first. Sit-down: pay after. Tableware is often ¥1–5 per person, extra.

How many free checks do I get?

3 free checks a day. A friend's link gets you 6. Watch a 5-second ad for +3 more, up to 5 ads a day. Paid unlock — see Register above.

What do I get when I pay $6.99?

One payment, 90 days — what's included and the payment status are in Register at the top of this page.

How much does a dish cost?

We don't list prices — they change too fast to keep right. Check the menu, or look the dish up on Dianping before you order.

How does inviting friends work?

Your link gives friends 6 free checks, and you get +3 for each friend who finishes their first check. Honest note: there's no server checking this yet, so sharing isn't the same as a verified invite.

Are the restaurant addresses verified?

No. The city lists show the type of place and the area — not a verified street address. Dianping has the real one.

Where can I find you?

We're #JadeBite on Instagram, TikTok and X — search any dish name there. Every dish has its own page too: chinabite.chinavisacheck.com/dish/mapo-tofu/
